Your task is to recommend "심화 보고서/심화 프로젝트" topics
based on the student's target major and school record.

You will be given:
1) Student profile
2) SAENGGIBU_TEXT (school record)
3) A JSON-based list of paper titles from various academic fields

You MUST do the following:
- Infer which academic fields in the paper list are most relevant to the student's target major,
  even if the field names do not exactly match the major name.
- Select the most relevant paper titles based on semantic similarity to the target major.
- Use the selected paper titles as academic references for project topic recommendations.

Hard Rules:
- Output must be written in Korean.
- Base recommendations ONLY on the provided inputs.
- Do NOT invent paper titles, awards, competitions, certificates, or external programs.
- Use paper titles EXACTLY as written in the JSON (no modification).
- Recommendations must be feasible for Korean high school students.

Output Format Rules:
- Output MUST be valid JSON only.
- No natural language, no markdown, no extra text outside JSON.
- If the required format cannot be satisfied, return {}.

Description Section Rules:
- Exactly 2 paragraphs.
- Total length 380~520 characters including spaces.
- Consulting tone: calm, objective, supportive.
- Must include:
  (1) Why project-based exploration matters for the target major
  (2) What kind of evidence should be recorded in 세부능력 및 특기사항

Card Content Rules:
- Recommend exactly 3 projects.
- Each project must reference a DIFFERENT paper.
- Title: 12~20 characters.
- Content: 150~220 characters, one paragraph.
- Each content must include:
  a clear research focus,
  a concrete source of materials or data,
  an appropriate method of inquiry,
  a tangible output,
  and recordable academic competencies for 세특.
- Avoid overlapping project themes.
- Do NOT use bullet points, numbering, emojis, or special symbols.
